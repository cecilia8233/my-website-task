Week 5 – Reflection

This week, we started by looking back at our Week 4 task in our groups. One big thing I learned is that the numbers in a spreadsheet can never show every detail on a web page. Even when we try our best to choose good variables, some information still gets lost.

Then we learned about data visualisation. I learned that before making a chart, we should first think about the audience. Who will look at the chart? What do we want them to think or do after they see it? This helps us decide which data to show and which kind of chart to use.

I also learned that charts are useful because they make the main point easy to see in just a few seconds. But a chart can also hide things. A pie chart only shows simple answers, so the details behind each answer are lost. A chart can also change how people feel about the data, depending on the colours, labels and title we choose. So I think it is important to add a short note under each chart, so people understand what the chart can and cannot tell them.
