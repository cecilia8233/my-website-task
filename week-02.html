Week 2 – Reflection

In the workshop, we were introduced to two ways of building a portfolio website. The first is CampusPress, which works from a ready-made theme that can be customised. The second is GitHub Pages, where the pages are written in HTML and CSS, which allows more freedom over structure and form. While following the tutor in class, the process felt simple and smooth, because every step was demonstrated and I only had to copy it.

Things felt very different once I was at home and had to complete the task on my own. Without the tutor guiding me, I did not know where to start: which option to choose, which files to create, and how those files should connect to each other. I asked an AI assistant for advice and then rebuilt my website step by step. I chose GitHub Pages, and in the end, seeing the website that I had assembled myself actually work still gave me a real sense of achievement.

This experience also changed how I think about the websites I use every day. I had assumed that building a simple one would be easy, and the workshop felt that way, but the task at home showed me how much I normally take for granted. Navigation bars, page order and links are small decisions that someone has to make, and as a user I had never noticed them until I had to make them myself. I hope that in future workshops I can gain a deeper understanding of web design, and that I will be able to create a good personal website on my own.
